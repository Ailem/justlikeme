Why this exists

Life insurance is one of the few financial products where the honest answer to "how much do I need?" is genuinely computable — yet almost nobody gets that answer without a salesperson attached to it.

JustLikeMe hands you the calculation directly. It runs the same needs-based method the industry uses — the one described in the NAIC's own buyer's guide — against published research, and shows its working. No commissions, no lead-selling, no policy recommendations. Just the arithmetic and the sources behind it, so you can walk into any conversation about coverage already knowing your number.
